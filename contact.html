Before you write

What kind of systems do you test? Anything where a model touches untrusted input or privileged actions: agents with tool access, retrieval pipelines, fine-tuning workflows, and the inference infrastructure underneath them.
Do you work through an NDA? Always, and I will sign yours. Published case studies only name clients who explicitly cleared it, and findings are summarized at whatever level you approve.
Can you work with our existing security team? That is the preferred arrangement. Most of my value is transferring method — your team should be able to rerun the testing after I leave, and that is what the handover is for.
Availability? Booking new engagements a quarter ahead. Short reviews and workshops can sometimes fit sooner, so ask.
