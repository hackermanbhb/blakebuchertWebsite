FAQ

Before you write

What kinds of systems?
Systems where a model touches untrusted input or privileged actions: agents with tool access, retrieval pipelines, fine-tuning workflows, and the inference infrastructure underneath them.
Do you work under an NDA?
Yes. Case studies name a client only with explicit permission.
Can you work with our security team?
Yes, and that is the preferred arrangement, so your team can rerun the testing afterwards.
